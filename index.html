Terms of Service

Payment

  • All commissions require full payment (100%) upfront or 50% after sketch and 50% after base color .
  • Payment is accepted via PayPal or platform-based payment (e.g. VGen).
  • Prices are listed in USD.
  • No refunds once the sketch has been approved.

Workflow & Revisions

  • Turnaround time is approximately 1–3 weeks depending on complexity and queue.
  • A sketch will be sent for approval before coloring.
  • Up to 3 minor revisions are allowed during the sketch phase.
  • Major changes after sketch approval or during coloring may incur additional fees.

Client Responsibility

  • Please include reference, pose, and preferred background when ordering.
  • Clear and complete references help ensure the best possible result.
  • Missing or unclear information may affect turnaround time.

Usage & Rights

  • Commissions are for personal use only unless stated otherwise.
  • Commercial usage requires an additional fee (+100% of base price).
  • I retain the right to display commissioned artwork in my portfolio and social media.
  • The client may not claim the artwork as their own.
